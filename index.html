Artist Bio

YAFFA is an 18-year-old rock guitarist based in New York City, known for her high-energy stage presence, melodic lead lines, and gritty, riff-driven sound. She’s already performed on major stages and has become a go-to session player for up-and-coming NYC bands.

Starting on classical piano at age 5, YAFFA spent nearly a decade building her musical foundation before falling in love with the guitar. Inspired by iconic rock performances and guitar heroes, she picked up a Stratocaster and began teaching herself through online videos before continuing with private lessons.


Since then, she has worked as a session guitarist and bassist for local NYC and Long Island artists, and played on stages across the city. On stage, she combines tight rhythm work with expressive leads; off stage, she is developing her own material as a solo artist, crafting songs that blend classic rock, modern punk, and glam-influenced guitar tones.
